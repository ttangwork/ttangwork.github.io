Hi, I'm Tommy.

I'm a DevOps engineer who's spent the better part of two decades in and around
enterprise IT — long enough to have watched "the server room" turn into "the
cloud console," and to have migrated more than a few legacy systems along the way.

I started out supporting business-critical applications and building ETL and
reporting pipelines for government and health, back when a good night's sleep
depended on a batch job finishing on time. Over the years I drifted toward the
infrastructure underneath it all: monitoring the mission-critical systems that
can't go down, and eventually building and migrating cloud platforms across AWS,
GCP, and Azure for banks, public sector, and infrastructure clients.

These days I spend most of my time in Terraform, Bicep, and CI/CD pipelines —
building platforms, automating the tedious parts, and helping teams get
comfortable running their own cloud infrastructure. I like the problems that sit
at the seams: where legacy meets cloud, where security meets delivery, where the
diagram meets what actually happens in production.

## Why /var/log?

On a Unix system, `/var/log` is where things quietly write down what happened —
the successes, the warnings, and the errors worth going back to read. This site
is my version of that: a technical journal where I log what I learn, the problems
I've worked through, and the occasional thing I want to remember for next time.

If something here saves you an afternoon of debugging, that's a win.
